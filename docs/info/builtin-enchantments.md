# Built-in Enchantments

EnchantmentReform 1.3.3 includes 132 enchantments for weapons, armor, tools, shields, fishing rods, and elytra. This page explains their default effects and how to activate them, so players can choose equipment and server owners can decide which enchantments to offer.

Values change with enchantment level: `10%/20%/30%` corresponds to levels I, II, and III, while `2–10` runs from 2 at level I to 10 at the maximum level. Health is measured in points; 2 points equal one heart. If your server has changed the settings, follow the in-game descriptions.

## How to get them

Most enchantments are available through enchanting tables, villager trades, or random loot, but each has its own sources. Double Jump and Jump Boost cannot be obtained at enchanting tables. Executioner and Shadow Guard are only available through villager trades. Applying an enchanted book at an anvil also depends on the enchantment’s supported items and conflicts.

Wear armor in its matching slot and keep weapons or tools in their configured active slot for effects to work. The equipment, maximum levels, and sources listed here are defaults. Server owners can change them in the [enchantment configuration](../configs/enchantment-configuration.md).

## Server versions

Entries without an extra requirement follow the plugin’s general minimums: Paper / Purpur 1.21.6+, or Spigot 26.1+, with Java 21+. “Paper” also includes Purpur and means the default effect uses a Paper-only event.

* Spear enchantments require Paper / Purpur 1.21.11+, or Spigot 26.1+.
* Rotten Flesh Tolerance and Fatigue Conversion require 26.2+.
* Impact Coil and Gale Impact require Paper / Purpur 26.2+.

Use Paper / Purpur 26.2+ for all 132 default enchantments. Of these, 16 depend on Paper-only triggers and cannot deliver their full effects on Spigot. The other 116 do not have that restriction but still require the versions listed in the tables. Folia support remains experimental; see [Requirements](requirements.md).
